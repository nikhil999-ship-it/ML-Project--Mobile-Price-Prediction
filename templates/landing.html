SPECIFICATION GUIDE

What Component Numbers Mean in Real Life

Understand key specifications without memorizing semiconductor technical manuals.

MEMORY

RAM (Operating Memory)

Determines how many applications stay alive in background without reloading.

4 GB: Sufficient for phone calls, basic messaging, and single-app use.
6 GB (Sweet Spot): Recommended standard for modern Android; zero lag on multitasking.
8 GB+: Reserved for heavy multitasking and demanding 3D games.
STORAGE

Internal Capacity (ROM)

Stores your operating system, downloaded apps, family media, and offline videos.

64 GB: Fills rapidly after 12 months of high-resolution WhatsApp media.
128 GB (Sweet Spot): Recommended for 2–3 year ownership without storage warnings.
256 GB+: Ideal if shooting 4K 60fps family video or storing large movie libraries.
BATTERY

Capacity Rating (mAh)

Quantifies total electrical charge stored within the internal cell.

4000 mAh: Acceptable for compact, lightweight handsets with moderate screen time.
5000 mAh (Standard): Provides full morning-to-night autonomy under active cellular use.
6000 mAh: Extended battery offering 1.5+ days runtime between charging sessions.
OPTICS

Camera Resolution (MP)

Sensor pixel count. Influences crop capability and digital detail rendering.

13 MP — 48 MP: Clean daylight snapshots for casual social sharing.
50 MP (Standard): Balances pixel-binning low-light sensitivity with sharp daytime detail.
108 MP+: Ultra-fine resolution allowing extensive digital zooming and cropping.
